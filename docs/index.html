📜 How to Play & Economy

1. Deposit: Send 1 Small First Aid Kit to HappyOkran[4427995] with the exact message dmanroll. Click below to auto-verify and receive 100 Game Gold.

2. The Game: Costs 100 Gold to start a run. Each roll adds (Die 1 + Die 2) × 5 to your Current Run. You can roll repeatedly without paying again.

3. The Danger: If you roll DOUBLES, you lose your entire Current Run and the run ends!

4. Bank: Secure your Current Run to your permanent Banked Total at any time before you hit doubles.

5. Withdraw: Send a Mail to HappyOkran[4427995] with the message withdraw [amount] (e.g., withdraw 500). The owner will manually send you the rewards in-game.
